Our Vision

About the club

To build a world-class robotics club, first in Hong Kong, then in Asia and the world, and to cultivate outstanding students who can take a real problem all the way to a working, reliable robot.

What We Work On

All projects

Everything in the club is a project, from application robots to competition entries.

🚁 Drones

Autonomous UAVs for building inspection and façade cleaning: flight control, positioning near tall buildings and safe operation.

🐕 Robot dogs

Quadruped robots that walk, perceive and navigate, including a robotic guide dog for visually impaired people.

🚗 Unmanned vehicles

Autonomous ground vehicles for campus delivery and patrol: LiDAR and camera perception, mapping and path planning.

🏆 Competition projects

Robocon and RoboCup projects that turn mechanical design, embedded control and autonomy into match-ready robots.

Latest News

All news
  • 2026 recruitment: 157 applications received September 2026Recruitment The 2026 recruitment campaign received 157 applications by 7 September. Applicants take a baseline written test and a structured interview. Successful students are placed into a member tier and one of the four technical groups, then complete the safety induction and sign the club rules.
  • Club showcase at PolyU Student Orientation 2026 1 September 2026Outreach The club welcomed new students at the PolyU Student Orientation with humanoid and quadruped robots, mobile robots and competition robots on display. Prof. Weisong Wen gave an information session on the club's vision and how students can take part.
  • Club reorganisation briefing at the Industrial Centre 25 August 2026Club Prof. Weisong Wen and the Industrial Centre briefed members on the club's new structure: three member tiers with public promotion criteria, four technical groups, a written proposal and oral defence for every funded project, and mandatory documentation and handover.
  • Two PolyU teams compete at the Robocon 2026 Hong Kong Contest 21 June 2026Competition PolyU entered two teams, PolyBotics and Redbricks, in the Robocon 2026 Hong Kong Contest, where 15 teams from 9 local universities and institutions competed on the “Kung Fu Quest” theme. Each team built a manual Robot 1 and an autonomous Robot 2 for weapon assembly, Kung Fu Scroll handling in the Meihua Forest and a tic-tac-toe finish.
