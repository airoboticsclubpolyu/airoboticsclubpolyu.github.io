What We Work On

All projects

Four directions, each tied to a supervised project or competition team.

🚁 Drones

Autonomous UAVs for building inspection and façade cleaning: flight control, positioning near tall buildings and safe operation.

🐕 Robot dogs

Quadruped robots that walk, perceive and navigate, including a robotic guide dog for visually impaired people.

🚗 Unmanned vehicles

Autonomous ground vehicles for campus delivery and patrol: LiDAR and camera perception, mapping and path planning.

🏆 Competitions

Robocon and RoboCup teams that turn mechanical design, embedded control and autonomy into match-ready robots.
