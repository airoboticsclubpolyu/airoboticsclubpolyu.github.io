How to apply and how members progress

The club recruits once a year at the start of the academic year. Beginners are welcome; everyone goes through the same steps and the promotion criteria are public.

How to join

  1. ApplyOnline application via the QR code on the recruitment poster.
  2. Written testA short baseline test of engineering and programming fundamentals.
  3. InterviewA structured interview about your interests and experience.
  4. PlacementA member tier and one of the four technical groups.
  5. InductionSafety induction for the Industrial Centre workspace.

Member tiers

Tier 3 · General Memberabout 80 places
Talks, workshops, demonstrations and introductory activities while you find a technical area.
Tier 2 · Training Memberabout 30 places
Structured training, practical tasks and contributions to club projects in at least one technical area.
Tier 1 · Advanced Memberabout 15 places
Competition teams and funded projects. Leads modules, mentors teammates and represents the club.

Promotion rules

Tier 3 to Tier 2
At least 70% attendance at relevant training over one semester, a passed foundation skills assessment, a contribution to at least one project, and a recommendation from a Tier 1 member or mentor.
Tier 2 to Tier 1
At least four of these six: a significant technical project or research contribution; a substantial contribution to a major competition; leading a sub-team or mentoring at least two members; at least 10 hours a week for one semester; high-quality documentation or presentations; two Tier 1 nominations with supervisor approval.
Staying in Tier 1
At least 80% attendance and continued delivery on your team's milestones.

Reviews, probation and appeals

  • Every member has a mid-semester check-in and an end-of-semester review.
  • Before anyone is moved down, they receive written notice, a mentor and an improvement plan, followed by a four-week probation.
  • Appeals can be submitted within one week with supporting evidence. Re-promotion is possible after one semester.
  • Safety comes first: repeated safety breaches can lead to suspension from the workspace and club activities.
