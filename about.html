PolyU FENG AI & Robotics Club

About

Who we are, what we do and how the club is organised

The PolyU FENG AI & Robotics Club is a student engineering community of the Faculty of Engineering at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. Members design, build and test real robots, from autonomous drones and legged robots to unmanned ground vehicles and competition robots, and learn how mechanical design, electronics, perception and AI come together in one working system.

The club is led by Ir Prof. H.C. Man, Dean of the Faculty of Engineering, and supervised by Prof. Weisong Wen with co-supervisors from AAE, EEE and ME. It works in the PolyU Industrial Centre, and postdocs and PhD students from the Trustworthy AI and Autonomous Systems Lab (TAS Lab) mentor members on technical work.

Our vision is to make robots that contribute to society, and to grow into a leading university AI and robotics club in Hong Kong and the Asia-Pacific region.

What we do

  1. Projects: supervised student projects on drones, robot dogs and autonomous vehicles, each with a written proposal, milestones and a final demonstration.
  2. Competitions: Robocon Hong Kong and ABU Robocon, with RoboCup preparation for 2028.
  3. Training: hands-on workshops on fabrication, embedded control, ROS, computer vision and safety.
  4. Exchange: industry visits, talks and outreach with PolyU and partner companies.

Technical groups

Mechanical Design
CAD, chassis and mechanisms, fabrication and assembly.
Electronics & Embedded Systems
Circuits and PCBs, microcontrollers, motor drivers, CAN bus and power.
Perception & Sensors
Cameras, LiDAR, IMU and positioning, sensor calibration and fusion.
Software & AI
ROS, planning and control, computer vision and machine learning.

Membership

Every member joins through the same application, written test and interview, and is placed into one of three tiers. Promotion depends on attendance, assessed skills and real project contribution, and the criteria are public.

Tier 3 · General Member
Talks, workshops and introductory activities while exploring a technical area.
Tier 2 · Training Member
Structured training, practical tasks and contributions to club projects.
Tier 1 · Advanced Member
Competition teams and funded projects; leads modules and mentors newer members.
