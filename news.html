September 2026Recruitment

2026 recruitment: 157 applications received

The refreshed recruitment campaign that opened in August 2026 received 157 applications by 7 September. Applicants take a baseline written test and a structured interview, and successful students are placed into the club's member tiers and four technical groups before a safety induction.

In its first year after the reorganisation, the club aims to build a core team of about 10 to 15 committed members, supported by a wider training group.

1 September 2026Outreach

Club showcase at PolyU Student Orientation 2026

With support from TAS Lab, the club welcomed new students at the PolyU Student Orientation with humanoid and quadruped robots, mobile robots and competition robots on display. Prof. Weisong Wen gave an information session on the club's vision and how students can take part.

The President of PolyU visited the booth, viewed the robots and met the student team.

25 August 2026Club

Club reorganisation briefing at the Industrial Centre

Prof. Weisong Wen and the Industrial Centre briefed members on the club's new structure: a tiered membership with public promotion criteria, four technical groups, proposal review for every funded project and competition team, and mandatory documentation and handover.

The 2026–27 plan focuses on Robocon 2027 and a small number of supervised seed projects on drones, legged robots and autonomous vehicles.

21 June 2026Competition

Two PolyU teams compete at the Robocon 2026 Hong Kong Contest

PolyU entered two teams, PolyBotics and Redbricks, in the Robocon 2026 Hong Kong Contest, where 15 teams from 9 local universities and institutions competed on the “Kung Fu Quest” theme. Each team built a manual Robot 1 and an autonomous Robot 2 for weapon assembly, Kung Fu Scroll handling in the Meihua Forest and a tic-tac-toe finish.

Neither team won an award this year. The design, build and integration work, and the lessons on reliability and rule compliance, are the starting point for the Robocon 2027 team.

5 June 2026Club

TAS Lab joins the supervision of the club

The Trustworthy AI and Autonomous Systems Laboratory (TAS Lab) began supporting the supervision of the club, led by Prof. Weisong Wen with support from the Faculty of Engineering, Ir Prof. H.C. Man and the PolyU Industrial Centre. TAS Lab postdocs and PhD students now mentor club members on drones, legged robots and autonomous vehicles.

2025–26 seasonTraining

Technical training workshops

During 2025–26, 58 students joined the club's training workshops on safety and tool use, SolidWorks and fabrication, soldering and PCB design, CAN bus, motor control and PID tuning, Python and OpenCV, and system integration. The practical target was a mobile robot that can pick up objects and shoot balls.

2025–26 seasonIndustry visit

Visit to the SMC smart factory

Members toured the SMC smart factory showroom to see pneumatic and automation components in industrial use, the same family of actuators used on competition robots.
