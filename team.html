PolyU FENG AI & Robotics Club

Team

Supervisors, mentors and technical groups

The club is guided by staff of the Faculty of Engineering and the Industrial Centre, with a PhD-level mentor team who work with students week to week on design, code and testing. Students work in four technical groups. Each group has a few student representatives who coordinate its work; these are working roles, not ranks.

Technical Groups and Student Representatives

Mechanical Design

CAD, structures and mechanisms, materials, fabrication, assembly and maintainability. Turns ideas into working robot bodies.

Mentors: Hu Jiahao

Student representatives: to be announced after the 2026 placement

Electronics & Embedded Systems

Power, wiring and PCBs, motor control, embedded computing and communications. Makes every subsystem run reliably together.

Mentors: Yang Mokui

Student representatives: to be announced after the 2026 placement

Perception & Sensors

Cameras, LiDAR, GNSS and IMU, calibration, state estimation, sensor fusion and mapping. Lets robots understand where they are.

Software & AI
