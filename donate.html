Donate and Partner

Help us build a club that lasts

The club gives students a place to design, build and test real robots and to represent PolyU in international competitions. We want this to be sustainable: robots wear out, parts break, competition rules change every year, and each cohort has to train the next.

Donations from alumni, families, companies and friends of PolyU help us keep equipment working, enter competitions and train more students. Every contribution, large or small, goes to student activities.

Who can donate
Individuals, alumni, companies and foundations
How
Contact us by email; donations are arranged through PolyU
Recognition
Sponsors are listed under Supporting Parties and Sponsors, with their agreement

What your donation supports

Robot parts and equipment

Motors, batteries, sensors, onboard computers, materials and spare parts for club robots.

Competitions

Entry, shipping and travel costs for teams representing PolyU, such as Robocon.

Training

Workshops, training kits and safety equipment for new members.

Long-term platforms

Robots that stay with the club, are documented and are improved by each new cohort.

Work with us: from student projects to industry

We welcome companies that want to bring a real problem to students, or to take club technology further.

Bring a problem

Share an inspection, logistics or service task; a student team can study it and build a prototype with mentors.

Sponsor a team

Support a competition team or a platform project, with your company named on the robot and on this website.

Visits and internships

Host company visits and offer internships to club members.

Put technology into use

Work with the club and PolyU to test and develop student robot technology in real settings.
