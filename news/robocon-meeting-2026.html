Robocon project meeting: competition plan, team roles and training

On 5 November 2026, the PolyU FENG AI & Robotics Club held a meeting to discuss its Robocon competition project. The team agreed on the competition plan and clarified the division of responsibilities for the work ahead.

The meeting also established a training and development plan to support members' technical learning alongside competition preparation.
