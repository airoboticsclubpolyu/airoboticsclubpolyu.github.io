2025–26 seasonTraining

Technical training workshops

During 2025–26, 58 students joined the club's training workshops on safety and tool use, SolidWorks and fabrication, soldering and PCB design, CAN bus, motor control and PID tuning, Python and OpenCV, and system integration. The practical target was a mobile robot that can pick up objects and shoot balls.
