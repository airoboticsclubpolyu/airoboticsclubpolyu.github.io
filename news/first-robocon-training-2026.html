First Robocon 2027 training: mechanical and software tracks

On 21 November 2026, the PolyU FENG AI & Robotics Club held its first Robocon 2027 training session. The session opened with an introduction to the competition, followed by training for students in the mechanical and software tracks.

The training connected the competition brief with mechanical design and software development for the team's robots. In the software session, students were introduced to a robot navigation example, showing how field configuration, simulation, path planning and robot control fit together.
