PhD-level mentors join the supervision of the club

Postdocs and PhD students from the Trustworthy AI and Autonomous Systems Laboratory (TAS Lab), led by Prof. Weisong Wen, began mentoring club members on drones, robot dogs and autonomous vehicles, with support from the Faculty of Engineering, Ir Professor H.C. Man and the PolyU Industrial Centre.
